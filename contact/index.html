To discuss a forensic video, demonstrative exhibit, mitigation film or commercial project, contact VarX directly. Email is the fastest way to reach us.

What to include

  • The location: where the site, property or incident is
  • What needs to be shown: the condition, sequence, person or footage the video has to make clear
  • How it will be used: deposition, mediation, sentencing hearing, trial, or a commercial project
  • Your deadline, and whether conditions on site may change soon (repairs, weather, demolition)
  • Any existing footage, photos, plans or expert reports the video should follow

Who VarX works with

Attorneys and litigation teams for either side, retained expert witnesses, and businesses that need commercial video. For pool, landscape and construction defect questions that need an expert opinion rather than a video, see exteriorart.com.
