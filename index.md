## Forensic services
Built for attorneys, litigation teams and expert witnesses.

- **Scene documentation:** on-site video and aerial documentation of properties, construction sites and incident locations, edited into clear visual timelines that follow the sequence of events in the case record and the retained experts' findings.
- **Mitigation videos:** documentary-grade films for sentencing hearings and settlement conferences: character testimony and context that paper briefs cannot convey.
- **Demonstrative evidence:** clear visuals that simplify complex technical subjects such as construction defects and mechanical failures.
- **Footage review and preparation:** frame-by-frame review, clarification and multi-camera synchronization of surveillance and cell phone footage.

## Why video evidence matters
1. **Clarity:** a technical sequence that takes pages to describe can often be shown in seconds.
2. **Grounded in the record:** every visual is built from site footage, photographs, plans and the retained experts' findings.
3. **Humanizing complex cases:** mitigation media gives judges context before sentencing decisions are final.
