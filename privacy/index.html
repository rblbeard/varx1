Privacy Policy

This policy covers the website varx1.com, operated by VarX in Southern California. Last updated November 7, 2026.

What the website collects

The site has no forms, accounts or logins and does not ask you to enter personal information. It is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs such as IP addresses for security and operation. The site does not use advertising or analytics trackers of its own.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and videos are embedded from YouTube and Vimeo. Those services may receive your IP address and may set cookies when you load or play content, under their own privacy policies.

Information you send us

If you email or call, we receive your contact details and anything you choose to share about your project, including footage, photographs and documents. We use it only to respond to you and to carry out work you hire us for. We do not sell, rent or trade it. Case materials are kept confidential and shared only with the people working on the project, with counsel, or as required by law or a court.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, subject to any legal obligation to keep it. California residents may have additional rights under California law.
